About Us

Our Journey as Student Entrepreneurs

As students selling palitaw, we started our small business to earn experience and learn the value of entrepreneurship. At first, it was challenging to balance our studies and business, but through teamwork, patience, and determination, we continued making and selling delicious palitaw. Every customer who buys our product inspires us to do better. This journey teaches us responsibility, hard work, and how to manage a business. More than earning money, we are proud to share a traditional Filipino delicacy with our customers while pursuing our dreams as students.

Filipino Food and Tradition

Palitaw is a traditional Filipino rice cake, boiled until it floats, then rolled in grated coconut, sugar, and sesame seeds. Tell customers what it means to your family.

Mission

Our mission is to provide delicious, affordable, and high-quality palitaw. As student entrepreneurs, we aim to develop our business skills, serve our customers with dedication, and learn the value of teamwork, responsibility, and hard work.

Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted student-led business known for delicious, affordable, and high-quality palitaw. We aspire to grow our small business, satisfy our customers, and proudly share Filipino culture and tradition through our products while gaining valuable entrepreneurial experience.

Why Choose Us

Enjoy our delicious, affordable, and freshly made palitaw with unique flavors. Every purchase supports our journey as student entrepreneurs!
